The Designer

Vrinda Sethi has never
drawn a garment she
couldn't make by hand.

Trained at NIFT Delhi and then, more usefully, on the floor of her grandmother's tailoring shop in Chandni Chowk, Vrinda opened the house in 2009 with four karigars and one pit loom. Seventeen years on there are a hundred and forty.

The house rule has not moved: no motif enters a collection until someone in the atelier has stitched it by hand at full scale. If it cannot be made well, it does not get shown.

Craft

A single Noor lehenga
takes nine hundred
hours on the frame.

Zardozi in Delhi. Chikankari in Lucknow. Bandhani in Bhuj. Kadhwa weaving in Varanasi. The house works with five clusters and pays by the piece, not by the hour, because good hand-work cannot be rushed into a shift pattern.

Every garment carries a card naming the karigars who made it.
